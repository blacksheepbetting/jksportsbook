A Letter to the FPHL: Building What Comes Next

The story of the Federal Prospects Hockey League and the people building a game without limits.

Josh King / 3min

Dear Players of the Federal Prospects Hockey League,

My name is Joshua King.

Prior to entering minor league hockey, I built my career managing successful ball and roller hockey clubs, where strong organization, top-tier branding, and robust NIL opportunities consistently attracted top talent.

When I attended my first FPHL game less than a year ago, I immediately saw untapped potential in the league. Since becoming an agent, I have built a strong roster—particularly for European players—by ensuring my clients earn top market value through comprehensive NIL programs, including upgraded housing and personal transport.

The FPHL has enormous potential as an independent league, unburdened by rigid, traditional systems. Moving forward, I am transitioning from agent work to team ownership. My goal is to build dynamic, high-energy rosters featuring players with distinct playstyles and strong individual brands.

I am hosting a segment on Inside the Fed, co-hosted by Shaun Weiss, where I will discuss these plans and the future of the league in more detail.

Best regards, Joshua King
