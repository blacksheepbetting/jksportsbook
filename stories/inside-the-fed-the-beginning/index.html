Inside the Fed: The Beginning

An invitation to an Indiana Sentinels game introduced Josh King to Single-A hockey and set him on an unexpected path through the FPHL.

Josh King / 3 min read / November 19, 2026

I started my second “career” as an agent less than a year ago.

It all started when Darrin Trebes, the number one pick in Indiana ball hockey, invited me to attend an Indiana Sentinels game. The last thing I wanted to do was attend a minor league hockey game.

Growing up, I was a huge Pittsburgh Penguins fan. Mario Lemieux and Jaromir Jagr were my favorites, just like every other kid growing up in Pittsburgh. Kovalev, Lang, Straka, and Kasparaitis were also among my favorites.

Hockey was part of my childhood. Fred Traynor, who was related to my stepmother, was the first general manager of the Wheeling Nailers. I loved going to Wheeling and experiencing that atmosphere.

A few years ago, though, I returned as a member of the media, and it just didn’t hit the same.

I’d always declined invitations to see the Indy Fuel. Quite honestly, I found it boring. So when I was invited to see the Sentinels, a team playing at an even lower level, I wasn’t exactly jumping at the opportunity.

But something happened that night.

Single-A hockey hooked me almost instantly. Not necessarily because of the level of play, but because of the presentation. The atmosphere, the experience, and the entire operation opened my eyes to something I hadn’t considered before.

I immediately saw the value in it, particularly because of the lack of NHL affiliation. I couldn’t believe this level of hockey existed, and I started looking at it through an entirely different lens.

Then I had a question.

Knowing Trebes could play, I couldn’t understand why he wasn’t on a roster. Why wasn’t someone giving him an opportunity?

That one question set me down the strangest, most unexpected path of my career.

What started as an invitation to a hockey game became something much bigger.

Welcome to Inside the Fed.

I’ll take you behind the scenes, introduce you to the people, tell you the stories, and show you a side of this world that no other member of the media gets to see.

We’re going places you won’t find anywhere else.
